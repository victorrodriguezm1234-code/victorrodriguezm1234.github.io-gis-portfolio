About

I'm from Arequipa, Peru, and I'm a graduate student in the Urban and Environmental Policy and Planning department at Tufts, studying sustainability. I did my undergrad in Vancouver, where I first got pulled into how the world fits together across borders — energy, cities, institutions, and the choices behind them.

What I love about GIS is that it gives the social sciences a way to speak visually. A policy, a migration, an inequality, a shift in land use — these are usually argued in words, but they all happen somewhere, and a map lets you actually see them. That's the thread through everything I do: using maps to turn ideas about energy, policy, and development into something you can look at and understand.
